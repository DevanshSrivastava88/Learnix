Privacy Policy

Learnix is a small task/habit tracker run by one person. This is the short, honest version.

What we store

  • Your Telegram user ID and first name
  • Your tasks and habits
  • Activity history (what you completed or skipped, and when)
  • Your settings (reminder times, streak, preferences)
  • Chat history with the bot — used to personalize reminders and understand your requests

Where it's stored

All data lives in Supabase, a managed Postgres database.

What we don't do

  • No ads
  • No selling your data
  • No third-party analytics or trackers

AI processing

To understand your messages, some of what you send may be processed by LLM providers — currently Groq and Google Gemini. This is only to interpret requests and generate replies, not for ads or model training on your data by us.

Deleting your data

Click "delete account" in the web app footer to permanently wipe everything tied to your account, or message the bot and ask.
